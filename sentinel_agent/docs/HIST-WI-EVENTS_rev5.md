# Historical well-integrity events (synthetic)

Dispositions were recorded by the shift at the time. Outcomes and labels were assigned afterwards.
The label source states how trustworthy each label is.

## Event W07-2023-04: A-annulus pressure drop while producing
- Observation: A-annulus about 40 bar below the expected thermal pressure during steady production.
- Disposition at the time: attributed to cooling after a choke change; no action.
- Outcome: three months later a tubing pressure test failed; a thread leak at about 1400 m was found.
- Label: tubing-to-annulus communication. Label source: confirmed by class-A test.
- Lesson: a low annulus pressure while producing can mean the annulus drains into the tubing.

## Event W03-2024-09: B-annulus rebuild after bleed
- Observation: B-annulus rebuilt from 5 bar to 22 bar within 6 hours after a bleed; extra gas returned.
- Disposition at the time: bleed-down and build-up repeated; gas sample taken.
- Outcome: gas composition matched a shallow formation, not the produced gas. Cement remediation planned.
- Label: sustained casing pressure in B from cement gas migration. Label source: confirmed by gas analysis and log.

## Event W05-2025-02: A-annulus rise after start-up
- Observation: A-annulus rose to 55 bar during the first 20 hours after start-up.
- Disposition at the time: bleed requested by the night shift.
- Outcome: the twin explained the rise as thermal; no rebuild after the bleed.
- Label: thermal pressure, no failure. Label source: operator disposition only, not verified by test.

## Event W01-2025-11: A-annulus transmitter flat line
- Observation: A-annulus pressure did not change for 9 hours, including during a bleed.
- Disposition at the time: none; the flat line was not noticed for two shifts.
- Outcome: the transmitter was found frozen; replaced.
- Label: loss of barrier monitoring. Label source: confirmed by instrument calibration.
