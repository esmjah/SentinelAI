# W01 A-annulus MAASP calculation report (revision B)

## 1. Purpose
This report sets the maximum allowable annulus surface pressure (MAASP) for the A-annulus of well W01.
Revision B was issued after the completion handover and covers the wellhead equipment only.

## 2. Basis
The A-annulus is bounded by the tubing-head spool and its outlet valves, the tubing hanger seals,
the production casing and the production packer. Revision B considered only the pressure rating of the
tubing-head spool and the A-annulus outlet valves, rated 5000 psi (344.7 bar).

## 3. Result
| Annulus | MAASP | Governing element |
|---|---|---|
| A | 345 bar | Tubing-head spool rating |

## 4. Known limitation
The packer differential and the tubing collapse load were not evaluated. A follow-up revision was
requested to include the downhole elements.
