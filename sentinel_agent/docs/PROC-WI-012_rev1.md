# Shut-in communication test (revision 1)

## 1. Purpose
Tests for communication between the tubing and the A-annulus by reversing the pressure difference
across the leak path.

## 2. Principle
While producing, the heated A-annulus can be above the tubing pressure at every depth, so a tubing or
packer leak drains the annulus. After a shut-in the tubing pressure rises above the annulus pressure
at almost all depths, so the same leak refills the annulus. A healthy annulus cools and its pressure
falls; a leaking annulus rises.

## 3. Steps
1. Agree the deferred production with operations; the test takes about 22 hours.
2. Shut in the well at the choke and the wing valve.
3. Record A-annulus and tubing-head pressure every 5 minutes.
4. Compare the A-annulus trend with the digital-twin expectation for a healthy well.

## 4. Interpretation
A rise of the A-annulus above the twin expectation indicates tubing to A-annulus communication. A
packer leak below the balance depth can instead show a lower-than-expected annulus. The test does not
locate the leak; a temperature or noise log is needed for the depth.
