# W01 well barrier schematic, production phase (revision 2)

## 1. Primary well barrier
Production tubing, production packer at 2300 m, downhole safety valve at 450 m with its control line,
and the production casing and its cement below the packer.

## 2. Secondary well barrier
Production casing above the packer, production-casing cement (top of cement 1200 m), tubing hanger and
seals, tubing-head spool with A-annulus valves, casing-hanger pack-off, master valve and wing valve.

## 3. Common elements
The production casing and its cement belong to both barriers at different depths. A single finding on
the production casing (for example wall loss) therefore affects both barriers.

## 4. Outer annulus
The intermediate casing and the casing-head spool with B-annulus valves contain the B-annulus. They are
not a well barrier in the NORSOK D-010 sense but protect against underground flow.

## 5. Monitoring
A-annulus and B-annulus pressure are monitored continuously at the wellhead. A permanent downhole gauge
at 2250 m measures tubing pressure and temperature.
