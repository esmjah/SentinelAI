# Study notes on IEC 61511 (internal summary, not the standard)

## 1. Safety instrumented functions
Protective actions such as closing the master valve and the downhole safety valve are performed by a
safety instrumented system designed, verified and maintained to a required safety integrity level.

## 2. Proof testing
Failures that only show on demand, for example a valve that does not close, are found by periodic proof
tests. The proof-test interval directly affects the probability of failure on demand.

## 3. Consequence for AI decision support
A data-driven model is not part of the safety instrumented function. It may advise, but it does not
replace or bypass the certified trip logic.
