# Study notes on 30 CFR 250.519-531 (internal summary)

## 1. Relevance
An example of a regulator making annulus pressure management mandatory. W01 is on the Norwegian shelf,
so these notes are for comparison only.

## 2. Content in brief
Operators must follow the industry recommended practice for casing pressure management and the
additional rules in these sections: how often casing pressure is monitored for each well type, when a
casing diagnostic test is required, how thermal effects after initial production are handled, when
tests are repeated, record keeping, and when corrective action or a casing pressure request is needed.
Where the recommended practice and the regulation conflict, the regulation applies.
