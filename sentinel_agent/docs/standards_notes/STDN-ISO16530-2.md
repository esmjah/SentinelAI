# Study notes on ISO/TS 16530-2 (internal summary, not the standard)

## 1. Scope
Guidance for managing well integrity during the operational phase: barrier monitoring, annulus
pressure management, testing, well operating limits and integrity categorisation.

## 2. Annulus pressure management
Each annulus has a maximum allowable annulus surface pressure (MAASP) based on the weakest element
exposed to it: casing burst, inner-string collapse, packer, wellhead or formation strength. Operating
limits and alert levels are set below the MAASP so that action is taken before the limit is reached.

## 3. Sources of annulus pressure
Thermal expansion, operator-imposed pressure and sustained pressure from a leak are distinguished.
Diagnostic bleed-down and build-up tests separate them.

## 4. Updating limits
MAASP values are re-evaluated after any change in well condition, such as wall loss found by
inspection or a change of annulus fluid.
