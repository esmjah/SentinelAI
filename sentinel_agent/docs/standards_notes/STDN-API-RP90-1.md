# Study notes on API RP 90-1 (internal summary, not the standard)

## 1. Scope
Management of annular casing pressure in offshore wells: thermal pressure, sustained casing pressure
and operator-imposed pressure; monitoring, diagnostic testing, maximum allowable wellhead operating
pressure (MAWOP) and documentation.

## 2. MAWOP
A conservative screen commonly taken as the lesser of half the burst rating of the casing evaluated and
80 percent of the collapse rating of the next inner string. Pressure above it triggers diagnostics and
an engineering review.

## 3. Diagnostic test
A bleed-down and build-up test shows whether pressure is thermal (stays low after the bleed) or
sustained (rebuilds). Fluid type and volume returned help to locate the source.

## 4. Risk-based management
Annuli outside the screening criteria can be managed by a documented risk assessment of the individual
well.
