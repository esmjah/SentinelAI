# Study notes on NORSOK D-010 (internal summary, not the standard)

## 1. What the standard covers
NORSOK D-010 sets minimum functional and performance requirements for well barriers over the whole
life of a well, from drilling to permanent abandonment.

## 2. Two-barrier principle
During production there shall be two independent, tested well barriers between the reservoir and the
environment. The primary barrier is the first envelope that stops flow from the source; the secondary
barrier is the back-up if the primary fails.

## 3. Well barrier elements
A barrier is made of elements such as tubing, packer, downhole safety valve, casing, cement, wellhead
and tree valves. For each element type the standard gives acceptance criteria: function, design,
initial verification and how it is monitored in operation.

## 4. Independence
Elements shared by both barriers (common elements) must be identified, and their failure assessed,
because one failure can remove both barriers.

## 5. Well barrier schematics
Each well has a schematic showing both barriers and their elements for the current well state.
